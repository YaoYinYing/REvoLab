# Provenance And Lineage

Use typed relations and Evidence resources. Pair a provider with every external ID. Treat artifact paths as locations, not identity. Resolve mutable run/artifact status through the owning driver when that capability exists.

Record provenance, warnings, and unresolved assumptions explicitly.
